## Brand & Style

This design system translates mystical nocturne luxury into a serene, radiant daytime presence. Inspired by morning sunlight cast over pearlescent ivory, champagne gold, and diffused lavender mist, it evokes an atmosphere of quiet opulence, intellectual clarity, and transcendent calm.

The visual direction merges **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- Generous, breath-filled whitespace anchored by tactile warmth rather than sterile digital whites.
- Fine jewel-like gold accents layered across soft cashmere tones.
- Glassmorphic overlays filtered through warm alabaster and milk-white diffusions, preventing high-contrast eye fatigue.
- Classical, contemplative authority expressed through serif editorial display paired with ultra-legible, human-centric sans body elements.

The target demographic values bespoke craftsmanship, modern spiritual wellness, high-end lifestyle curation, and quiet wealth. The aesthetic balances reverent mystique with accessible daylight usability.

## Colors

The palette transforms morning illumination into functional digital surfaces, avoiding blinding #FFFFFF in favor of mineral, organic undertones.

- **Primary (`#D4A017`)**: Solar champagne gold. Used for key call-to-actions, focal badges, fine focus borders, and elevated moments of status.
- **Secondary (`#6366F1`)**: Velvet iris / mystic dawn amethyst. Employed for esoteric markers, interactive toggles, highlights, and subtle atmospheric glows.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`)**: Morning sage / crystalline teal. Reserved for positive confirmation, wellness metrics, balanced badges, and tranquil notifications.
- **Neutral Base & Canvas**:
  - Pearlescent Canvas (`#FBF9F5`): The primary viewport background, soft and gentle on the eyes.
  - Cashmere Surface (`#F4EFEA`): Primary container and card surface.
  - Alabaster Shading (`#EAE3DA`): Subtle borders, structural dividers, and secondary component wells.
- **Neutral Typography (`#1E1B2E` / `#2D283E`)**: Dawn charcoal with rich violet-plum undertones, providing deep contrast without harsh pure-black starkness.

## Typography

The typography couples the intellectual dignity of **EB Garamond** with the clean, modern warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy**: EB Garamond provides classical cadence, evoking heritage publications, esoteric scrolls, and illuminated books. Set italic variants selectively on introspective quotes or emphasis words.
- **Body & UI Roles**: Plus Jakarta Sans delivers pristine clarity across small viewports, forms, and data structures. It prevents fatigue and balances the expressive serif display.
- **Uppercase Labels**: Small tags and category markers are set in semi-bold or bold sans-serif with tracked spacing (`+0.04em` to `+0.1em`) to mimic architectural inscriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs an expansive, unhurried spatial layout based on an 8-point baseline rhythm. Generous margins and breathing rooms reinforce a feeling of tranquility and quiet luxury.

- **Grid Model**: 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1320px`), reflowing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.
- **Spatial Principles**:
  - Density is avoided. Elements prefer vertical padding to allow Garamond's ascenders and descenders breathing room.
  - Section dividers rely on generous vertical spacing (`space-2xl`) combined with delicate, hairline separators rather than heavy structural cuts.
  - Component layouts favor asymmetric, curated balance to reflect high-end editorial art direction.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is achieved via translucent luminescence and diffused dawn light, rather than industrial drop shadows.

- **Alabaster Glassmorphism**: Translucent card backgrounds (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.72)`) with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` layer over ambient gold and lilac light washes.
- **Pearlescent Halos**: Ambient shadows are warm, low-contrast, and deeply feathered:
  - *Tier 1 (Resting)*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 40, 62, 0.04)` combined with a hairline champagne border (`rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.15)`).
  - *Tier 2 (Floating / Active)*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(45, 40, 62, 0.08)`, enriched by an inner border reflection (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
  - *Tier 3 (Overlay / Modal)*: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.14)`.
- **Specular Highlights**: Top edges of cards and navigation surfaces feature a subtle 1px border gradient transitioning from shimmering champagne gold (`rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.3)`) to milky ivory (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness tier of `2`, yielding an organic, tactile, and sculptured feel that softens the architectural structure of the layout.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard inputs, actionable chips, select buttons, and secondary badges.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px)**: Content cards, persistent drawers, and modular dashboard containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px)**: Hero visual cards, dialog surfaces, and prominent notification modals.
- **Pill (Fully Rounded)**: Reserved strictly for floating status tags, primary aesthetic action pills, and floating navigation bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Solar Button**: Solid champagne gold (`#D4A017`) fill with dawn charcoal text (`#1E1B2E`), semi-bold Plus Jakarta Sans, rounded-md (`8px`) or rounded-pill. Subtle gold ambient shadow on hover (`0 4px 14px rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Mist Button**: Pearlescent ivory surface with a delicate 1px border in gold tone (`rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.4)`), text set in `#2D283E`. Translucent gold background tint on hover.
- **Tertiary Ethereal Ghost**: Transparent background with iris violet or charcoal link text, accompanied by an animated underline that expands from center outwards.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from cashmere surface (`#F4EFEA`) or warm milk glass (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.78)`).
- Outlined with 1px soft pearl borders (`#EAE3DA`) or champagne micro-strokes.
- Headlines inside cards utilize EB Garamond for an editorial gallery experience.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact and refined with micro-typography (11px, `tracking-wider`, uppercase).
- Tinted subtly with secondary iris (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`) or sage (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)`), framed by matched tone-on-tone borders.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Ivory-tinted base (`#FBF9F5`) with cashmere border (`#EAE3DA`). On focus: transition to 1px glowing gold border (`#D4A017`) accompanied by a subtle iris-tinted glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`). Label positioned in clean medium-weight Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular or soft-square frames tinted in alabaster, transitioning to solid champagne gold fill with pure white glyphs upon selection.

### Lists & Dividers
- Hairline rules (`1px`) painted with soft gradient masks that fade out at the viewport edges (`rgba(234, 227, 218, 0.8)`).
- List elements utilize generous spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`), pairing serif titles with sans-serif descriptive metadata.
